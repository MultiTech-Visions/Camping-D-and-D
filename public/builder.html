Story Arc Builder

Plan out your game ahead of time — the characters, the places, and the story — then send it back as one file to load into the game. No internet needed, and everything you type saves right here as you go.

How this works (start here)
  • You don't need internet, and you can't break anything. Add a little whenever you have a minute — your work saves automatically in this page, so you can close it and come back later. When you're happy with it, you'll send one file back to be loaded into the game.
  • You build your game out of “cards.” Each card is one piece of your world — a Character or Monster, a Place, or a Story beat (a moment in the plot). Add as many as you like, in any order. Don't worry about getting it perfect; you can always come back.
  • Your storyline goes under Story beats. Inside a story beat you can lay out chapters, and inside each chapter the individual scenes — the moments of the story, in order. This is where your story arc comes together, so jot down plenty of short scenes.
  • Everything you type is either shown to your players or kept just for you. Use the private notes for anything only you should know — secrets, reminders, ideas you're still chewing on. Use the normal fields for the things you want your players to see.
  • During the game, the things you choose to show appear on a big screen (a projector) for everyone at the table. If there's no projector set up, your players can browse that same information on their phones instead. Your private notes never appear in either place — they stay with you.
  • Pictures: if you'd like artwork, just describe the picture you want — who or what's in it, the mood, the setting. When your file is submitted it goes through an AI process that creates the art for you, so you don't have to make anything yourself.
  • When you're done (or just want to send what you have so far), tap Export at the top and email or share the file that downloads. That's it.
